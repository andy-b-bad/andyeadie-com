Clause F15.1–F15.3

Overtime

  • Rate: hourly overtime is paid at 1/3 of the daily Performance Salary, subject to a maximum of £88 per hour or part thereof.
  • Daily limit: overtime is limited to four hours per day; any additional overtime is subject to local discussion.
  • 15-minute grace: artists shall work 15 minutes overtime without payment to complete a “take” when requested at the end of the working day or night. If overtime extends beyond those 15 minutes, the 15-minute period is included for payment purposes.
  • These overtime payments are not included in the Artist’s Total Applicable Salary.
  • The overtime provisions may be varied where an Artist’s weekly Performance Salary exceeds £4,767.
