Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?

What does this tool do?

Drawing only from the documents we upload, the app answers relevant questions in seconds. It furthermore preserves a detailed trail of what was asked and what the system returned - in addition to user ratings, cost, performance and other auditable data.

How do i use it?

Log in here and ask it a question!

How does it get the correct answer?

A specialist type of search reasons over the document as a human would - navigating hierarchy, pulling exact traceable references, and using those references while generating the answer.

Why don't I just ask ChatGPT or Gemini?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok etc use vast amounts of publicly available data to predict the closest answer to a question, often approximating or 'hallucinating' wrong answers in the process - unnaceptable.

This tool does not invent, it pulls only from the documents we upload for it. If the answer is not covered by them, it will say so.

Does it keep a record of what I ask?

Yes. The system keeps a record of user questions and ratings, app behaviour and responses in a secure database. This can be used to help with quality control, cost auditing and automated self-improvement cycles.

However! Emails are anonymised so your questions are not linked to your identity.

Why do I have to login?

To protect the app from abuse, and record questions and answers (not linked to your email) for ongoing improvement cycles.

Is this a finished product?

No. The app is a working prototype. Fully functional front and secure back end, but with only one operational contract - PACT CINEMA. I'm seeking funding to upload the other contracts, extend functionality, improve visual styling, automated reports etc.
